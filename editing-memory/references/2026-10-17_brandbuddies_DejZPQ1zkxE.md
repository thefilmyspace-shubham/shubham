## 2. Editing principles extracted (the "why", not the copy)

1. **Keep the shot long, change the layer.**
   - Presenter shots are long: about 4.4 s on average and up to 6.5 s, 13 cuts in 58 s.
   - The text changes every **1–1.5 s**, so the eye always gets something new without jump-cut fatigue.
2. **One outfit, many locations.** 8 presenter setups (gate, kiosk, graffiti wall, plaza, table, arch, dark wall, street, railing), one per script beat. Each new sentence group gets a visual reset without needing B-roll. Shot size also cycles: medium → MCU → wide → seated → walking → close.
3. **Typographic hierarchy inside one sentence.**
   - Connector words ("We", "Here's", "choose", "us", "to", "about") are tiny and thin.
   - Each sentence has **one hero word** that is huge and heavy, in white, yellow, neon green, chrome or texture fill.
   - Small words sit around the hero word like an editorial layout rather than a caption line.
   - It reads like captions but looks like design.
4. **Text behind the subject.** The hero word sits *behind* her head or hair (person cut-out on top). Instant depth, and it ties the text to the person.
5. **Entrances and exits are part of the language.**
   - Small words type in letter by letter.
   - Hero words blur → sharp in about 0.12 s.
   - On exit, words desaturate to grey, blur and fade instead of simply cutting.
6. **Explain with metaphor graphics, not stock B-roll.**
   - Headcount pile-up = cost and complexity.
   - Lines into one node = "manage the process".
   - 18-tile grid with lit tiles = "patterns".
   - Filling bar = "performs".
7. **Each graphic pops on the exact spoken noun.** Cards in the explainer appear on "strategist", "script writer", "editor" and so on.
8. **Proof beats claims.** Real client ad creatives appear exactly on "turn them into ad creatives".
9. **Sound restraint.**
   - The music bed sits about **10 dB under the voice** (music ≈ -26 dB, voice ≈ -16 dB average). It is a steady loop of about 80 BPM.
   - Only about 8 loud SFX hits in 58 s (≈3.8, 8.2, 8.8, 10.0, 22.2, 23.5, 25.2, 52.8 s), on cuts and reveals only.
   - **The music cuts out under the CTA**, so "DM us" lands in near-silence.
10. **The CTA is shown as a UI action, not just said.** WhatsApp pill → cursor click → chat window shows the viewer exactly what to do.

## 4. Weak spots (do not copy)

- **Readability:**
  - "complicated" (24 s) and "Brands" (47 s) are texture-filled over busy backgrounds and hard to read.
  - "Already working in your niche" overlaps her face for a moment.
- **Repeated motif:** the green progress bar is used twice within 5 s (≈45 s and ≈49 s). The second use feels like a template habit.
- **Long graphics blocks:** two 8–9 s full-screen graphics blocks with no presenter. That works here because each block pays off a promise, but 8 s is the upper limit for a 58 s reel.
- **Price in the hook:** effective for them. **Not allowed for TFS** (Shubham: "price mt dikhana").
