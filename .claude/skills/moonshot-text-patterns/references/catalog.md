### Eclipse (`ca-split`)

- Colours: #FFFFFF #FFE500
- Words per line: Up to 4
- As you speak: A colour block sits behind the spoken word
- Behind the speaker: yes · AI Edit (scene changes/B-roll): no · Plan: Pro
- Best for: statement hooks, founder takes and personal-brand clips where one big word should sit behind your head
- When to use: Use Eclipse, the flagship behind-the-person style, for hooks that need one unmissable word. The key word locks behind your head in a heavy condensed face while subtitles ride your chest in front, turning a plain talking head into a poster-like frame. It suits founders, coaches and educators.
- Skip when: Skip it when you sit far from the camera or the background right behind your head is busy; the effect needs a clear silhouette.

- Look: Hindi and roman in one face; hero word behind you.
- Fonts: Baloo 2 ExtraBold
- Colours: #FFFFFF #FFE14F
- Words per line: Up to 4
- As you speak: The spoken word flips to the highlight colour
- Behind the speaker: yes · AI Edit (scene changes/B-roll): no · Plan: Pro
- Best for: Hindi and Hinglish videos that mix Roman and Devanagari in one caption
- When to use: Use Desi Bold for Hindi and Hinglish clips. Hindi and Roman text share one face, Baloo 2 ExtraBold, so a mixed sentence looks consistent, and the hero word locks behind you. It suits Indian creators in education, finance, comedy and commentary who switch scripts mid-sentence.
- Skip when: Skip it for English-only clips that want a lighter editorial feel, and when the frame lacks a clear silhouette for the behind effect.
- Thumbnail: `moonshot-templates/11-Desi/01-Desi-Bold.jpg`

- Fonts: Baloo 2 ExtraBold + Anton
- Colours: #FFFFFF

## Social

### Floodlight (`social-yellow`)
- Look: Yellow box, black ink on the key word.
- Fonts: Montserrat ExtraBold
- Colours: #FFFFFF #FFE500
- Words per line: Up to 3
- As you speak: A colour block sits behind the spoken word
- Behind the speaker: no · AI Edit (scene changes/B-roll): no · Plan: Creator
- Best for: everyday Reels, TikToks and Shorts that need clean, punchy, readable captions
- When to use: Use Floodlight for maximum legibility. Black ink in a yellow box marks the key word, in Montserrat ExtraBold, three words at a time. It reads instantly on any background, which makes it a strong default for tips, news, reactions and clips watched on mute.
- Skip when: Skip it if the look must feel premium or quiet. The yellow box is loud by design and familiar from many feeds.
