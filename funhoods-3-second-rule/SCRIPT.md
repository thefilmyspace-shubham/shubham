# Funhoods — "The 3-Second Rule" · explainer reel · VO script v1 (draft for approval)

**Format:** the editing language of the reference reel (`references/insta_DdyjJkcNoSR/EDIT_BREAKDOWN.md`), **with no A-roll at all**.
Every shot is graphics, cut about every 2 s:
- white-background typography that builds up word by word into a bold **blue** keyword, with a gold highlighter bar;
- collage cutouts (our product cut-outs + the dealer character from the Dino shop story);
- UI mock-ups with cursor clicks;
- **black & white "meme" inserts with bold Hinglish captions**. These use our own illustrated characters, never copyrighted film or meme clips, because this is a client ad.

**Audience and language:** B2B (distributors & wholesalers) · no phone number · no price · English VO, Hinglish meme captions ·
about 60 s · 9:16 · 4K60.

**Rule: the VO never speaks a sound effect.** Clicks, pops, whooshes, the friction whirr and the kids' "wow" are SFX, synced to the motion.

## VO (English, curious explainer tone → confident, about 150 words)

```
Some toys sell out in one day... and some sit in your godown till next Diwali.
So what's the difference? Three simple rules.
Rule one: kids decide in seconds.
If a toy needs batteries, a remote or a manual... it has already lost.
But one push... and the Funhoods Dino Car takes off. No batteries needed.
Rule two: the wow moment.
It flips a full 360... and the dino mouth opens wide.
Our Press and Go Scooter? Press the head, it turns all the way round... and off it goes.
Rule three: the box sells before you say a word.
Twelve Dino Cars in a bright display box. Scooters in a see-through pouch. Kids see the toy, not just the packet.
Now, the part that matters to you.
BIS approved. Safe for little hands. Packed for bulk: 192 Dino Cars or 240 scooters per carton... with pan India delivery.
So, distributors and wholesalers... which toy in your godown hasn't moved since last Diwali?
Replace it with Funhoods. Bulk orders open now.
Funhoods... Childhood Full of Fun!
```

## Facts used (each from the client's material)
- **Dino Car (MT-03):**
  - push & go, no batteries needed (client's own VO);
  - 360° tumbling stunt car;
  - "DINOSAUR MOUTH CAN BE OPENED UP AND DOWN";
  - 12 pcs display box, 192 pcs per carton;
  - BIS approved, safe for little hands.
- **Press & Go Scooter (PL-001):**
  - press the head, the head turns 360°, it goes forward;
  - new transparent pouch;
  - 240 pcs per carton;
  - BIS IS 9873, 100 % safe for children, non-toxic.
- **Not claimed:** "Made in India" for the whole range (only the scooter's label says it); any price; any sales numbers.
- **The "sell out in one day" lines are the story's framing,** not a statistic.
