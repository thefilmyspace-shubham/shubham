# Problem Discovery Format

Koi naya business ya nayi niche aaye toh pehle yeh karo. Goal hai owner ke **roz ke problems** dhoondna, features nahi. Features baad mein `niche-automation-planning-format.md` mein plan honge.

Ek niche ke liye kam se kam **3 owners** pe yeh process chalao. Ek owner ki problem uski apni ho sakti hai, 3 mein same aaye toh woh niche ki problem hai.

---

## Golden rules

1. **"Kya chahiye?" mat poochho. "Kal kya hua?" poochho.** Owner ko solution nahi pata hota, par apna din yaad hota hai.
2. **Call mein solution mat batao.** Bas suno aur likho. Pitch alag din.
3. **Har manual cheez ek problem hai.** Register, notebook, sticky note, calculator, Excel, WhatsApp pe hisaab, "main yaad rakh leta hoon".
4. **Teen tarah ka dard hota hai:** paisa (nuksaan ya leak), time (ghante jaate hain), tension (gussa, darr, bhool jaana).
5. **Teen logon se baat karo:** owner, ek staff member, ek customer. Teeno alag problems batayenge.
6. **Number maango.** "Kitni baar?", "Last time kab?", "Kitna nuksaan?" Bina number ke problem asli nahi maani jaayegi.

---

## Step 1: Pehle 15 minute ki research (visit se pehle)

| Kya dekhna | Kahan | Kya nikalna |
|---|---|---|
| Business ke 1–2 star Google reviews | Google Maps | Customer kis baat pe naraz hai: wait, bill, behaviour, booking |
| Woh abhi jo app use karta hai uske bad reviews | Play Store | Existing software kahan fail ho raha hai |
| Niche ke owners ke groups | Facebook, WhatsApp, Reddit, YouTube comments | Common rona kya hai |
| Competitor apps ke feature list | Unki website | Kya already solved hai |

---

## Step 2: Observe karo (1–2 ghante, peak time pe)

Busy time pe jaake chupchaap dekho. Inme se jo bhi dikhe, likho:

- [ ] Koi kaam **do baar** ho raha hai (pehle likha, phir type kiya)
- [ ] Customer **wait** kar raha hai ya line lag rahi hai
- [ ] Staff har baat pe **owner se pooch raha hai**
- [ ] Owner ko kitni baar **beech mein bulaya** gaya
- [ ] Phone ya WhatsApp pe kitni **calls/messages** aaye, kis baare mein
- [ ] Paisa kaise liya, **hisaab kahan likha**
- [ ] Koi cheez **khatam** hui ya dhoondhni padi
- [ ] Kahan **galti** hui (galat bill, galat order, bhool gaye)
- [ ] Koi **register, notebook, ya chit** use ho rahi hai
- [ ] Staff khaali baitha hai ya bahut bhaag raha hai

---

## Step 3: Owner interview (30–45 minute)

Din ke time ke hisaab se poochho. Har jawab pe dig karo (Step 4).

**Subah dukaan khulte hi**
- Aate hi pehla kaam kya karte ho?
- Kya check karte ho? Kaise?
- Staff aaya ya nahi, late hai, kaise pata chalta hai?

**Din mein**
- Sabse busy time kab hota hai? Us waqt kya galat hota hai?
- Customer sabse zyada kis baat pe naraz hota hai?
- Kaunsa kaam aapke bina nahi hota?

**Paisa aana**
- Aaj kitna aaya, kaise pata chalta hai?
- Cash aur UPI ka hisaab kaise milate ho?
- Udhaar kitna baaki hai, kisse? Yaad kaise dilate ho?
- Pichle mahine kitna paisa aisa tha jiska hisaab nahi mila?

**Paisa jaana**
- Supplier ko payment kaise track karte ho?
- Kharche kahan likhte ho?

**Staff**
- Salary kaise calculate hoti hai? Chhutti, late, overtime, commission?
- Kaun kya kaam kar raha hai, kaise pata chalta hai?
- Staff pe bharosa kis cheez pe nahi hota?

**Customer**
- Purane customer wapas aate hain? Kitne?
- Unka number save hai? Kabhi unko message karte ho?
- Booking, appointment ya order kaise lete ho? Phone pe, WhatsApp pe?

**Stock aur supplier** (agar maal hai)
- Maal kab order karte ho? Kaise pata chalta hai khatam hua?
- Kuch kharab ya expire hota hai? Kitna?

**Raat ko band karte waqt**
- Band karte waqt kya karte ho? Kitna time lagta hai?
- Ghar jaake bhi kaam ka kya sochte ho?

**Mahina end**
- GST, CA, salary, rent, renewal, mahine ke end mein kya bhaag-daud hoti hai?

**Magic questions (yeh zaroor poochho)**
- Pichle hafte sabse zyada gussa kis baat pe aaya?
- Agar aap ek hafte chhutti pe chale jao toh kya toot jaayega?
- Kaunsa kaam aap khud karte ho jo kisi aur ko dena chahte ho?
- Aapko kahan lagta hai paisa leak ho raha hai?
- Pehle koi app ya software try kiya? Kyun chhoda?
- Agar ek problem kal se khatam ho jaaye toh kaunsi chahoge? Uske liye mahine ka kitna doge?

---

## Step 4: Dig karna (har problem pe)

Owner kahe "haan yeh problem hai", toh yeh 5 sawaal poochho:

1. Kitni baar hota hai? (roz / hafte / mahine)
2. Last time kab hua? Kya hua tha?
3. Kitna nuksaan hua? (₹, ghante, ya customer gaya)
4. Abhi kaise handle karte ho?
5. Usse kya fark padta hai? (yeh 2–3 baar poochho, asli wajah tak pahuncho)

**Example:** "Customer renewal bhool jaate hain" → kitne mahine mein? "10–15" → kaise yaad dilate ho? "Khud call karta hoon jab yaad aaye" → kitna time? "Haftey ke 3 ghante" → nahi yaad aaya toh? "Customer chala jaata hai, ₹2,000 ka" → **asli problem: har mahine ₹20,000–30,000 ka renewal leak + 12 ghante.**

---

## Step 5: Staff aur customer se baat (10 minute har ek)

**Staff se**
- Sabse irritating kaam kya hai?
- Owner kis baat pe daantta hai?
- Kya cheez yaad rakhni padti hai?
- Kahan galti ho jaati hai?

**Customer se**
- Yahan aane mein kya pareshani hoti hai? (wait, booking, bill, reminder)
- Kabhi kuch bhool gaye, ya inhone bhula diya?

---

## Step 6: Problem log (sab ek jagah likho)

| # | Problem | Kisko (owner/staff/customer) | Kitni baar | Nuksaan (₹ / ghante / tension) | Abhi kaise handle | Proof (dekha / suna / review) | Frequency 1–5 | Dard 1–5 | Paise se link 1–3 | Score |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | | | | | | | | | | |

**Score = Frequency × Dard × Paise se link.** Max 75.

- 45+: top problem, isi se shuru karo
- 20–44: second wave
- 20 se kam: abhi chhod do

**Proof ka rule:** jo sirf "suna" hai woh weak hai. Jo "dekha" ya review mein mila, woh strong hai.

## Step 7B: "Mana karna" points (auto-enforcement)

Har business mein kuch jagah owner ya staff ko kisi ko **"na" bolna padta hai** ya **police karna padta hai**. Yeh sabse achhe automation points hain, kyunki system khud rule laagu kare toh na jhagda, na leak, na sharam.

Gym ka example: membership khatam hote hi door ka fingerprint kaam karna band kar deta hai. Owner ko member se kuch bolna nahi padta.

**Owner se yeh poochho:**
- Kahan log bina pay kiye service le lete hain?
- Kisko "aapka time khatam ho gaya" ya "pehle payment karo" bolna padta hai? Kitni baar?
- Kaunsa rule hai jo staff follow nahi karta? (discount, free item, late entry)
- Kahan aapko khud khade hoke check karna padta hai?
- Kis baat pe customer ya staff se jhagda hota hai?

**Log karo:**

| Rule jo toot-ta hai | Kaun todta hai | Kitni baar | Nuksaan | Abhi kaun rokta hai | Auto kaise rok sakte hain |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| | | | | | |

**Rokne ke 3 tareeke (sasta pehle):**

| Level | Tareeka | Cost | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Sirf software | App mein red/green, booking block, payment link pehle | ₹0 | Fees pending toh coaching app pe test lock |
| 2. Software + staff | Entry pe staff QR scan kare, red aaye toh andar nahi | ₹0, staff ka phone | Gym reception pe member QR |
| 3. Software + device | Device khud rokta hai: door lock, turnstile, locker | ₹8,000–35,000 ek baar | Gym door fingerprint |

Pehle Level 1 ya 2 try karo. Level 3 tab jab leak ka nuksaan device ki cost se zyada ho.

**Niche examples (shuruaati andaza):**

| Niche | Rule | Auto-enforcement |
|---|---|---|
| Gym | Membership expire = entry band | Fingerprint/face door lock software se jude, ya reception QR red |
| Coaching | Fees pending = access band | Student app/test lock, attendance pe owner ko alert |
| Salon | Package khatam = free service band | Booking pe auto "balance zero, pay first" |
| Restaurant | Discount ya bill cancel bina permission | Owner ko WhatsApp OTP, bina OTP discount nahi |
| Kirana | Udhaar limit cross | Billing pe credit limit block |
| Clinic | Follow-up free sirf 7 din | System 7 din ke baad fee auto lagaye |
| Hostel / PG | Rent pending | Door access card band, WhatsApp reminder pehle |

---

## Step 8: Validate karo

Top 3 problems wapas owner ko batao:

1. "Maine yeh 3 problems note ki. Kya yahi sabse badi hain? Kuch chhoota?"
2. "Agar inme se ek main solve kar doon, toh kaunsi pehle?"
3. "Uske liye mahine ka ₹___ theek lagega?" (niche ke hisaab se number bolo, phir unka reaction dekho)

**Rule:** 3 mein se 2 owners same problem ko top 3 mein rakhein aur paise ke liye haan bolein, tabhi woh asli problem hai.
