# Funhoods — PVC Racing Car (PVC-3301) · VO script v2 + storyboard (draft for approval)

Reel: **32 s** · 9:16 · white-background motion graphics (same system as the Dino Car / Fighter Jets reels) · B2B
(distributors & wholesalers only) · no phone number · real photos/videos are reference only.

Catalogue (FUNHOODS_02.pdf, p. 25, "Upcoming"): **PVC Racing Car · PVC-3301 · outer carton 36 pcs · Inertia car** (free wheel).
The catalogue picture shows it in orange and in blue. No certification is listed for it.

Product facts, from the box and the demo videos in Drive (1 Nov):
- RACING INERTIA CAR, friction / hand push / inertia ("FULL SPEED AHEAD")
- FOUR-WHEEL DRIVE · STURDY · COOL STYLE · 3+ AGES (the box also prints "multiple models / colors", but this SKU comes in one colour only)
- LOUD MUSIC · BRIGHT LIGHT: the functional version, NO.3301 (ticked on the box), runs on 3 × 1.5 V Ag13 button batteries.
  "Pull out the barrier and music and lights will come on". The demo video shows the headlights glowing when the car is pressed.
- Left out on purpose:
  - "no batteries" (the lights and music use button batteries)
  - BIS / Made in India (neither is printed on this box)
