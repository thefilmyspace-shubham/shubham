# Petpooja (Prayosha Food Services Pvt Ltd): Pricing Format, Business Model and Company Economics — state as of 1 Nov 2026

Scope note: Petpooja publishes INR prices only to visitors it geolocates to India; from a non-India IP the official pricing page shows USD. All INR figures below therefore come from (a) competitor/third-party pages that reproduced the India page in Sept–Oct 2026, (b) software directories, and (c) older reseller listings. Each figure carries its observation date and a reliability flag. Research tools could not reach the Wayback Machine, so historical snapshots of the INR page were not retrievable.

## Key Question 1 — Core POS subscription pricing (per outlet, annual vs monthly, enterprise, setup, renewal)

### Takeaway
Petpooja is priced per outlet, per year, with three software tiers; the India base (POS-only) licence was listed at ₹12,000 + 18% GST on petpooja.com as of Oct 2026, with Growth/Scale bundles at ₹20,000–₹40,000 per outlet per year. Directory listings still show the older ₹10,000 first-year / ₹7,500 renewal anchor, and the international page shows $50–$150 per outlet per month, confirming an annual-first model with a monthly option only outside India.

### Cited Findings
- Official page structure (observed 1 Nov 2026, served from a US IP): plans Core, Growth, Scale (plus a "Basic" column in the feature table); toggles "Monthly price, billed annually" and "Monthly price, billed monthly"; no INR shown; footer says "Local pricing may vary based on your region & currency. Contact us for accurate, localized rates." — [petpooja.com/pricing](https://www.petpooja.com/pricing)
- Official USD prices on the same page (observed 1 Nov 2026 via direct download of the HTML): Core $50, Growth $95, Scale $135 "per month/per outlet" billed annually; Core $65, Growth $115, Scale $150 per month/per outlet billed monthly. Page text: "With the Scale and Growth plan, you can choose any option you like" (add-ons are pick-and-choose on upper tiers). — [petpooja.com/poss/pricing](https://www.petpooja.com/poss/pricing)
- India INR ladder as reproduced from petpooja.com/pricing by DineOpen (competitor), checked 21 Oct 2026, "exclusive of applicable taxes": Base Plan (POS) ₹12,000; Operations Manager Growth ₹20,000; Operations Manager Scale ₹30,000; Marketing Manager Core ₹20,000; Marketing Manager Growth ₹30,000; Marketing Manager Scale ₹40,000. Billing period not stated on the page per DineOpen. — [DineOpen pricing 2026](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/petpooja-pricing-plans-2026.html); corroborated by [DineOpen review 2026](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/petpooja-review-2026)
- SoftwareSuggest listing (pricing "last updated 2 July 2026", shown in USD, "+18% GST, billed yearly"): Electron Poss Core $133, Growth $222, Scale $333; Android Poss $100; Poss Lite $67; Local Poss Core $222, Local Poss Growth $333, Local Poss Scale $444. Growth = everything in Core + 1 visit/year + "any 5 marketplace services"; Scale = 2 visits/year + captain app, KDS, table reservations, API, Tally/SAP/ERP integration. ### Inferences
- The SoftwareSuggest USD ladder ($67/$100/$133/$222/$333/$444) is almost certainly an INR list converted at ~₹90/$: Poss Lite ≈ ₹6,000, Android Poss ≈ ₹9,000, Electron (cloud) Core ≈ ₹12,000, Growth ≈ ₹20,000, Scale ≈ ₹30,000, Local (on-premise) Core/Growth/Scale ≈ ₹20,000/₹30,000/₹40,000. The ₹12,000/₹20,000/₹30,000/₹40,000 points match DineOpen's Oct 2026 reproduction of the India page exactly, so the current (mid/late-2026) India list price for the cloud POS base is ₹12,000/outlet/year + GST (≈₹14,160 incl. GST), up from the long-running ₹10,000.
- Techjockey's ₹10,000 Core figure is probably a lagging listing (or a channel discount); Techjockey's "Save upto 18%" banner refers to GST input credit, not a discount.
- US pricing implies an annual-plan premium of ~15–30% for monthly billing (Core $50 vs $65); if India mirrors this, a monthly option would cost roughly ₹1,150–₹1,300/month for the base plan, but no INR monthly figure has been observed anywhere.
- A "Lite" tier (~₹6,000/yr) and an "Android Poss" tier (~₹9,000/yr) exist below the cloud Core in the mid-2026 catalogue, i.e., Petpooja now has an entry point below ₹10,000 — relevant to anyone planning to undercut it.

### Gaps
- No direct observation of the live India INR page was possible (geo-gated); the ₹12,000/₹20,000/₹30,000/₹40,000 ladder rests on a competitor's reproduction (DineOpen, 21 Oct 2026) plus the SoftwareSuggest USD conversion.
- Year-2 renewal price under the 2026 ladder is unpublished; the ₹7,500 renewal figure is from older listings and may no longer apply.
- No published enterprise/chain contract pricing, volume discounts, or formal setup fee schedule.
- No INR monthly plan price observed.

## Key Question 2 — Add-on pricing (online ordering, inventory, CRM/loyalty, captain app, KDS, QR ordering, WhatsApp, reservations, feedback, payroll, etc.)

### Takeaway
Petpooja does not publish per-add-on INR prices; add-ons are bundled into the Growth/Scale tiers (Growth = "any 5 marketplace services", Scale = all) or "quoted separately per outlet". Credit-based items (WhatsApp, e-invoice, SMS) are metered and bundled in fixed quantities per tier.

### Cited Findings
- Official add-on list on the pricing page (1 Nov 2026): Kitchen Display System, Captain ordering app, Restaurant website, Petpooja Loyalty, Token Management System, Feedback App / QR, OOW (online ordering website), QR Ordering / Digital menu; footer also lists Scan & Order, Self-Service Kiosk, Wireless Calling Device, SMS Service, Order Reconciliation, Analytics & Insights. No prices shown. — [petpooja.com/pricing](https://www.petpooja.com/pricing)
- Inventory module, in-built CRM, 80+ reports, central kitchen module, menu management are listed as core features (not separately priced add-ons). — [petpooja.com/pricing](https://www.petpooja.com/pricing)
- Growth tier includes "any 5 marketplace services" and one on-site visit/year; Scale includes two visits/year plus captain app, KDS, table reservations, API, Tally/SAP/ERP (SoftwareSuggest, Jul 2026). — [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/petpooja)
- WhatsApp marketing credits bundled: 4,000 / 8,000 / 10,000 by tier; 1,000 e-invoice credits in Operations Scale; Marketing Scale (₹40,000) includes "Meta ads or two influencers" (Oct 2026). — [DineOpen](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/petpooja-pricing-plans-2026.html)
- Payroll (now "Attendo"), Invoice, and Tasks (now "Stepwise") are separate Petpooja products; Techjockey lists "Invoice by Petpooja, Payroll by Petpooja, Tasks by Petpooja" inside the Scale POS tier. — [petpooja.com](https://www.petpooja.com/); [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/petpooja-pos)
- Waiter Calling Device is marked "(AMC)" on SoftwareSuggest with no price (i.e., hardware with annual maintenance). — [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/petpooja)
- Knomi (competitor, Oct 2026) states all add-ons (Captain app, KDS, QR ordering/digital menu, loyalty & SMS CRM, online ordering website, feedback app) are "quoted separately per outlet" with no published price; it uses ₹4,000/yr per add-on as an illustrative placeholder only. — [Knomi](https://www.knomi.in/compare/petpooja-pricing-breakdown)
- Competitor OCHI (undated, updated 31 Oct 2026) claims add-on figures such as accounting sync ₹1,500/month, delivery-platform connection ₹2,000/platform, SMS ₹0.25/message, custom reports ₹5,000 setup + ₹500/month, cloud storage ₹500/month — the post cites no sources, mixes Indian and Moroccan examples, and is internally inconsistent; treat as unverified. — [OCHI blog](https://blog.ochi.ma/petpooja-pricing-features-pricing-ochi-alternative)

### Inferences
- The practical add-on economics are: ~₹8,000/outlet/year to go from Base (₹12,000) to Growth (₹20,000) for five modules, and ~₹18,000/year to Scale (₹30,000) for everything. This implies an effective price of roughly ₹1,500–₹2,000 per add-on per year when bundled — far below the ₹4,000-per-add-on placeholders used by competitors.
- Metered credits (WhatsApp, e-invoice, SMS via DLT) are the main usage-based upsell inside the subscription; top-ups beyond the bundle are an unpublished revenue line.

### Gaps
- No individual INR price for any single add-on was found on petpooja.com or any listing; the "quoted per outlet" model means prices are negotiated.
- No price for Petpooja Loyalty, reservation manager, feedback app, or online-ordering website as standalone items.

## Key Question 3 — Hardware pricing (POS machines, printers, Android devices, bundles)

### Takeaway
Petpooja sells hardware as a separate, quote-only package and explicitly supports bring-your-own devices (iOS/Windows/Android, standard thermal/A4 printers). The only concrete bundle price found is a dated reseller listing: Android POS with built-in printer + 1-year Petpooja subscription for ₹18,000 (MRP ₹25,000), renewal ₹5,000.

### Cited Findings
- Official FAQ: Petpooja POS "works smoothly with iOS, Windows, and Android laptops, computers, tablets and phones" and supports regular A4 and WiFi/LAN/USB/Bluetooth/dot-matrix/laser/label printers; no hardware prices on the page (1 Nov 2026). — [petpooja.com/poss/pricing](https://www.petpooja.com/poss/pricing)
- Techjockey FAQ: hardware "is not included… a separate package that must be bought, with pricing available by requesting a call." — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/petpooja-pos)
- IndiaMART reseller listing: "Android POS Machine" combo of Android POS hardware (in-built printer, 1-year warranty) + Petpooja subscription at ₹18,000/piece first year (struck-through ₹25,000), renewal ₹5,000, excl. taxes; listing ~1,400 days old (circa early 2023). — [IndiaMART](https://www.indiamart.com/proddetail/android-pos-machine-2848970029330.html)
- Petpooja's hardware-integration glossary: supports thermal printers from Epson, TVS, Bixolon, KDS screens, barcode scanners, weighing scales, UPI terminals; POS Local setup guide says customer arranges the machine and Petpooja's team handles installation and outlet mapping. — [blog.petpooja.com hardware integration](https://blog.petpooja.com/glossary/hardware-integration/)
- Third-party estimates (not Petpooja quotes): thermal printer ₹3,500–₹8,000; total hardware ₹15,000–₹50,000+; competitor claim that Petpooja steers new signups to a recommended tablet+printer bundle adding ₹8,000–₹25,000 — all from competitor blogs. — [dineomAI](https://dineomai.com/blog/petpooja-pricing); [DineOpen](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/petpooja-pricing-plans-2026.html); [OCHI](https://blog.ochi.ma/petpooja-pricing-features-pricing-ochi-alternative)
- Attendo (payroll) is sold "with biometric hardware available as a combo" (no price). — [petpooja.com](https://www.petpooja.com/)
- Payment hardware (EDC machines, QR soundbox) is supplied via partners Paytm, Razorpay, Pine Labs, Mosambee, BharatPe under "Pay+"; no device prices disclosed (blog, 3 Jul 2026). — [Petpooja Finance blog](https://blog.petpooja.com/industry-business-guides/petpooja-finance-money-layer-in-pos/)

### Inferences
- Hardware is a pass-through/attach revenue line rather than a core margin driver: Petpooja's own positioning (BYOD, standard printers) suggests it avoids proprietary-terminal lock-in, and the ₹18,000 combo (≈₹10,000 software + ≈₹8,000 Android device) implies thin hardware margin at reseller level.

### Gaps
- No official 2025–2026 price for any Petpooja-branded or recommended POS machine, printer, KDS screen, soundbox or EDC device.

## Key Question 4 — Transaction-based revenue (payments MDR, online-order %, aggregator commissions, lending, marketplace margins)

### Takeaway
Petpooja states it takes zero commission on aggregator (Zomato/Swiggy) integration; its payments layer ("Pay+"/Petpooja Finance) routes to third-party PSPs whose fees are shown per transaction but not published by Petpooja, and it offers "Petpooja Loans" working capital plus an HDFC current-account referral — so transaction/fintech revenue exists but is undisclosed and appears small relative to subscriptions.

### Cited Findings
- Official FAQ: "Petpooja charges zero commission on integrating any third-party aggregator with your restaurant POS"; only the aggregator's own fees apply. — [petpooja.com online order management](https://www.petpooja.com/poss/online-order-management-software)
- Petpooja Finance / Pay+ (blog, 3 Jul 2026): card, UPI and QR acceptance via connected EDC/QR devices; partners Paytm, Razorpay, Pine Labs, Mosambee, BharatPe; dashboard shows "provider charges next to the final credited amount"; auto-settlement of orders on payment confirmation; Soundbox voice confirmation; "Petpooja Loans" described as an existing working-capital option across POSS; HDFC Bank current-account application via the Finance Marketplace; full HDFC business-loan journey "on the roadmap". No MDR rates, fees or pricing stated. — [Petpooja Finance blog](https://blog.petpooja.com/industry-business-guides/petpooja-finance-money-layer-in-pos/)
- A Petpooja-branded page on the food-court product pitches "Petpooja Pay" to sync all payments in one app. — [petpooja.com food court POS](https://www.petpooja.com/poss/food-court-and-canteen-pos-software)
- Early company history (secondary source): revenue streams identified as "1. Marketplace providing useful and integrated services for restaurants 2. Transaction-based revenue from online and credit card transactions." — [Digi-Corp blog](https://www.digi-corp.co/blog/from-an-idea-to-profitable-venture-story-of-petpooja/)
- Scale of order flow (a lever for any future take-rate): Entrackr says Petpooja "processes about 25% of online order volumes on Zomato and Swiggy" (29 Sep 2025); the official pricing page states "40%… Online Orders Processed on Zomato & Swiggy" (1 Nov 2026); Inc42 says ~7 Mn orders/day (Sep 2025). — [Entrackr](https://entrackr.com/news/petpooja-raises-155-mn-series-c-at-over-3x-valuation-premium-10512396); [petpooja.com/poss/pricing](https://www.petpooja.com/poss/pricing); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/petpooja-bags-inr-137-cr-to-automate-day-to-day-restaurant-operations/)
- Competitor claims of "transaction fees of 1.5–2% on online orders" and "2.5–3.5% gateway fees" are unsourced; Knomi explicitly says the MDR is set by the gateway agreement, not Petpooja. — [DineOpen alternatives](https://www.dineopen.com/blog/petpooja-alternative-2026); [Knomi](https://www.knomi.in/compare/petpooja-pricing-breakdown)
- An older Petpooja marketing deck promoted "Lowest MDR rates" and "Low transaction cost" for UPI, without figures. — [Scribd (old deck)](https://www.scribd.com/document/400633122/Petpooja-The-most-connected-Restaurant-POS-platform-in-town)

### Inferences
- With FY25 revenue of ~₹102 Cr and ~1 lakh restaurants, revenue per restaurant is ~₹10,000/year — essentially the base subscription — which implies payments/lending/marketplace contribute little today; the fintech layer launched/expanded in mid-2026 is the main untested upside in Petpooja's model.
- Petpooja's revenue from PSP partners is most plausibly a referral/revenue-share on MDR rather than Petpooja-set pricing; nothing published confirms the split.

### Gaps
- No disclosure of payments GMV, take rate, loan volumes, or marketplace commission; FY25 filings do not break revenue by stream in any source found.

## Key Question 5 — Free trial / freemium / lifetime offers

### Takeaway
There is no free tier or lifetime plan; Petpooja sells via a free 10-minute demo, and only one directory mentions a 7-day trial.

### Cited Findings
- Official site offers a free "10-minute demo" booking; no free trial or free plan mentioned (1 Nov 2026). — [petpooja.com](https://www.petpooja.com/); [petpooja.com/pricing](https://www.petpooja.com/pricing)
- SoftwareSuggest mentions a 7-day free trial in one place while its FAQ says trial details "haven't been shared" (Jul 2026). — [SoftwareSuggest](https://www.softwaresuggest.com/petpooja)
- Techjockey: no free trial mentioned; offers "Buy Now & Pay Later". — [Techjockey](https://www.techjockey.com/detail/petpooja-pos)
- Old Petpooja deck titled "Free Restaurant Management Software" exists on Scribd (pre-2019 marketing, not current). — [Scribd](https://www.scribd.com/document/400633122/Petpooja-The-most-connected-Restaurant-POS-platform-in-town)

### Inferences
- Demo-led, sales-assisted onboarding (with included online installation/training) is the acquisition motion; the absence of self-serve trial means a competitor offering genuine self-serve/freemium would be differentiated on go-to-market, not just price.

### Gaps
- Whether a trial is offered informally by sales/resellers is unknown.

### Inferences
- Implied FY23 revenue ≈ ₹53 Cr (from the 43% FY24 growth); growth decelerated from ~43% (FY24) to ~32–34% (FY25).

## Key Question 7 — Go-to-market (field sales, resellers, aggregator partnerships, ONDC)

### Takeaway
Petpooja sells through demo-led direct sales supported by included installation/training, a formal reseller/referral program paying 15% of first-year revenue, and distribution via 300+ integrations (Zomato, Swiggy, payment partners); ONDC is referenced but no partnership terms are published.

### Cited Findings
- Official reseller program (India page, observed 1 Nov 2026): resellers "earn 15% of assured revenue" per conversion, credited within 10–12 days after client onboarding; KYC needs Aadhaar, PAN, cancelled cheque, GST number; existing POS users get a flat ₹500 referral bonus; "No hidden charges". — [petpooja.com/poss/reseller-program](https://www.petpooja.com/poss/reseller-program)
- Partner-directory listing describes 10% ongoing commission while the referred client remains active, and elsewhere 20% for the lifetime of active clients, across reseller/VAR/distributor/SI roles (terms and dates unclear, conflicts with the official 15% one-time figure). — [Elioplus](https://elioplus.com/profiles/vendors/31298/petpooja-prayohsa-food-services-pvtltd)
- Growth attributed "mainly to pricing and word of mouth"; founder Parthiv Patel: "In terms of website traffic, I guess only GST and IRCTC are ahead of us." (Feb 2025). — [The CapTable](https://the-captable.com/2025/02/petpooja-saas-restaurant-software-foodtech-cloud-kitchen/)

Zomato, Swiggy, UPI, FreeCharge, HDFC Bank; zero-commission aggregator integration; POS migration help (CRM data and aggregator re-setup) included at onboarding. — [The CapTable](https://the-captable.com/2025/02/petpooja-saas-restaurant-software-foodtech-cloud-kitchen/); [petpooja.com/pricing FAQ](https://www.petpooja.com/pricing)

### Inferences
- At 15% of a ~₹12,000 licence, a reseller earns ~₹1,800 per new outlet — cheap acquisition for Petpooja, suggesting blended CAC well under one year of subscription revenue; the heavier cost is the ~1,780-person headcount for support and on-site installation.
- The Zomato/Swiggy order-integration position (25–40% of aggregator order volume) is the moat a clone must replicate, since aggregator API partnerships, not price, drive switching costs.

### Gaps
- No disclosed sales headcount, number of active resellers, share of revenue via channel vs direct, or CAC/payback figures.
- ONDC partnership scope and any revenue terms are undocumented.
