# Funhoods — Dino Car (MT-03) · "The Shop Story" · VO script v1 (draft for approval)

Reel: about 33 s · 9:16 · 4K60 · animated entirely in code (flat illustration + the real Dino Car cut-outs) ·
B2B: distributors & wholesalers only · no phone number · no price.

**Idea:** a quiet toy shop gets the Funhoods Dino Car display box. Kids stop, push it, see the 360° flip and the
chomping mouth, and crowd in. By evening the box is empty and the dealer reorders. The message for distributors is
simple: put it in every shop on your route, and the shops keep coming back.

**Rule: the VO never speaks a sound effect.** No "roar", "zoom" or "vroom" in the voice. The dino roar, the real
friction whirr, the shutter, the truck and the crowd are separate sound effects in the mix.

## VO v1 (English, warm storyteller → excited, about 85 words, about 32 s)

```
[warm] Morning at the toy shop. Full shelves... but nobody stops.
[excited] Then the Funhoods Dino Car arrives!
Twelve dinos in a bright display box, right on the counter.
One push, and off it goes! A full 360 flip... and that dino mouth opens wide!
Kids crowd in... and by evening, the box is empty. Time to reorder!
Four colours, BIS approved, safe for little hands.
192 pieces per carton, pan India delivery.
Distributors and wholesalers, put the Dino Car in every shop on your route!
[excited] Funhoods... Childhood Full of Fun!
```
