# Funhoods — "Daana's Journey" · brand storytelling reel · VO script v1 (draft for approval)

**Story:** the journey of one tiny plastic granule, **Daana**, at the Funhoods factory in Ratlam:
1. graded and logged;
2. moulded on Funhoods' own tooling and coloured;
3. hand-assembled;
4. drop-tested;
5. BIS certified, packed and shipped;
6. a child picks it up in a toy shop.

Then the twist: Daana has 100+ siblings (the whole range). Then why distributors pick Funhoods, and a closing line that hands the story to them.

**Format:** the reference reel's editing language (`references/insta_DdyjJkcNoSR/EDIT_BREAKDOWN.md`), **with no A-roll**.
Every shot is graphics, cut about every 2 s:
- white background, build-up typography → one bold **blue** keyword, a gold highlighter bar;
- collage cut-outs of the real products;
- simple UI / process cards;
- **black & white meme inserts with bold Hinglish captions**, drawn with our own characters (no film or meme clips; this is a client ad).

Daana is a cute illustrated granule with big eyes, drawn in code. He changes colour as the story goes.

**Audience and language:** B2B (distributors & wholesalers) · no phone number · no price · English VO, Hinglish meme captions ·
about 65 s · 9:16 · 4K60.

**Rule: the VO never speaks a sound effect.**

## VO (English, warm storyteller → confident, about 170 words)

```
This tiny grain of plastic is about to become a child's favourite toy.

Daana is born at Funhoods, in Ratlam. Pure virgin polymer... never recycled.
First, he's checked, graded and logged.

Then the scary part: a one-metre drop onto a hard floor. He survives.
Lab-tested, BIS certified, packed in a standard carton... and off he goes from Ratlam.
Days later, in a toy shop, a little girl spots him... and doesn't let go.
But Daana isn't alone. He has more than a hundred brothers and sisters.
Rattles for babies. Press and go vehicles. Builder sets. Military sets. Learning boards.
And new trending toys, every month.
So why do distributors choose Funhoods?
Everything is made in-house, so there's no middleman markup.
Made in India, so no import delays.
BIS approved, so your customers trust it.
And fixed carton counts keep your stock simple.
Daana's journey ends in a child's hand. The next one can start from your godown.
Funhoods... Childhood Full of Fun!
```

## Facts used (all from the client's catalogues: "Wholesale Catalogue 2026" (Oct) and FUNHOODS_02)
- **Brand:** Toy Manufacturer · Made in India · Ratlam · 100+ SKUs · BIS approved.
- **Process:**
  - Selection: virgin polymer granules graded and batch-logged;
  - Moulding: in-house injection moulding on our own tooling;
  - Colouring: colour-fast masterbatch, no fading, no bleeding;
  - Assembly: hand-assembled and function-checked piece by piece;
  - Packing: standard cartons, fixed counts, labelled for stock control;
  - Shipping: dispatched from Ratlam across the dealer network.
- **Quality:** every batch lab-tested, every product BIS certified · melt-flow tested · colour-fast · drop test: withstands 1-metre drops onto a hard surface · non-toxic, virgin polymers only, never recycled.
- **Categories:**
  - Rattles: baby-safe, colourful, musical;
  - Vehicles: press & go, push-and-play, pull back;
  - Builder Series: construction toys, real replicas;
  - Military Sets: press-to-shoot, multi-piece;
  - Educational: learning, maps, peg boards;
  - Trending: latest releases.
- **Why Funhoods (for dealers):**
  - in-house manufacturing (no middleman markup);
  - Made in India (no import delays, no currency risk);
  - BIS approved (child-safety certification you can promote);
  - standard carton sizes (fixed outer counts make stock management simple);
  - rapidly expanding range (100+ SKUs, growing every month).
- **Not used:**
  - phone number, prices, credit/scheme terms;
  - products outside the October catalogue (e.g. the PVC Racing Car, whose box says Made in China);
  - the word "metal".
