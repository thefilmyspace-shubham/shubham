Lines 2 and 7 (mugs/glasses, wooden tray) become B-roll with on-screen text only, and she doesn't speak them. Everything else stays.

## 3. Shoot notes
- **Presenter look:** an elegant ethnic suit or kurta with a dupatta in a jewel tone (maroon, bottle green or royal blue), light jewellery. The Begum look should match the Nawabi theme.
- **Delivery:** confident with a little nakhra (attitude). Line 3 (saasu maa) is the main laugh, so give it a pause and a voice change.
- **Framing:** the same as the reference. Talking head at the counter with a product in hand, a B-roll cut on every product name, and the RC logo shot at the end.
- **Confirm with the client before the shoot:**
  - Exchange offer terms: which old utensils they take (steel, aluminium, brass?), which new products it applies to, and how long it runs. This decides the on-screen text in line 8.
  - "Candies" is read here as the candy jars and dry-fruit bowls (their own hashtags are #candyjar and #dryfruitcandy). If they meant something else, change line 4.
